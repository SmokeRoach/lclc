Bag fees are where cheap flights stop being cheap. Here's what each airline actually lets you take for free. All sizes come from the airlines' own help pages, **checked October 2026**. Airlines change these, so check your booking before you fly.

## Ryanair
- **Free:** one small personal bag, **40 × 30 × 20 cm**, that fits under the seat in front.
- **Paid:** "Priority & 2 Cabin Bags" adds a 10 kg bag (55 × 40 × 20 cm) for the overhead locker and priority boarding. Or add a 10 kg check-in bag, which you drop at the desk.
- **The catch:** if your bag doesn't fit the sizer at the gate, it gets refused or put in the hold for a fee.
- Ryanair bumped the free bag up from 40 × 25 × 20 cm in late summer 2025. Older bags sold as "Ryanair size" might be the old size, so measure yours.

## easyJet
- **Free:** one small under-seat bag, **45 × 36 × 20 cm** including wheels and handles, up to 15 kg.
- **Paid:** a large cabin bag (56 × 45 × 25 cm) comes with Speedy Boarding. easyJet says it's always cheaper online than at the gate, where it costs **£60**.
- easyJet's free bag is roomier than Ryanair's, so a bag that's fine on easyJet may fail on Ryanair.

## Jet2
- **Free:** one cabin bag up to **10 kg and 56 × 45 × 25 cm**, plus a personal item up to **40 × 30 × 20 cm**.
- That's a lot more than the other two. Factor it in when you compare a Jet2 fare with a "cheaper" Ryanair one.

## Our rules for never paying at the gate
1. **Buy one bag that fits the smallest allowance** (Ryanair's 40 × 30 × 20 cm). Then it works on all three.
2. **Measure it packed.** Soft bags bulge. Wheels and handles count.
3. **Wear the bulky stuff.** Coat, hoodie, trainers. Pockets are free.
4. **Add bags when you book,** not at the airport. It's always the most expensive at the gate.
5. **Two of you? Compare one hold bag between you** against two sets of cabin upgrades. It's often cheaper.
