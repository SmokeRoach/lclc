Refunds & cancellation

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Cancel any time

Log in to Whop, go to Profile → Orders, choose your Low Cost Legends Club membership and select Cancel membership. You'll get a confirmation email. You keep access until the end of the month you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again. Stuck? Email smokeroach0151@gmail.com and we'll sort it.

Your 14-day right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 you normally have 14 days after buying to cancel and get a refund. Because membership gives you instant access to digital content, at checkout we ask you to agree that access starts straight away and that you understand you lose the 14-day right to cancel once you start accessing the content. If you haven't accessed the members' area, you can still cancel within 14 days for a full refund.
